# Anyone But You
My favorite movie is **Anyone But You** starring *Sydney Sweeney* and *Glen Powell*. 

### Overview
**Anyone But You** is a 2023 romantic comedy film about two people who can’t stand each other but pretend to be a couple during a destination wedding to keep up appearances. As they fake their romance, their old sparks of dislike turn into unexpected real attraction. 

### Main Cast 
1. Sydney Sweeney as *Bea*
2. Glen Powell as *Ben*
3. Charlee Fraser as *Margaret*
4. Alexandra Shipp as *Claudia*

## Why I like it
The reason why I like it because I really love their chemistry in that movie and I'm a fan of Sydney Sweeney. If i were to rate the movie, it is a 10/10 movie for me and it is a must watch if you're into rom/com genre.
